1. Project Vision Statement
1.1 Vision Statement
The group discussion and the initial consultation with the clients assisted in determining the following vision statement, which will be put in our virtual team room to remind everyone in the team on the right path to go and motivate them to complete the project.

WHO medical imaging professionals and researchers who need to take their OCT annotation functionality off-desktop and into the cloud THE OCT Web Annotation Tool is a browser-based GUI application with the proven ability to provide a smooth cloud-based annotation experience, providing freehand and keypoint annotation tools, customisable surface label palette with pre-defined ordering, pan and zoom on volumetric high-resolution images and JSON output,
1.2 Problem Context
Medical image annotation is a fledgling research and practice in the field of ophthalmology. Images taken by Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) generate a volumetric image of the retina and the accurate visualization of the boundaries of retinal surfaces such as the inner limiting membrane (ILM) and the retinal pigment epithelium (RPE) is necessary to:
    • Derivation of clinical measures (e.g retinal layer thickness)
    • Generation of ground-truth data to be used to train AI and machine learning models.
    • Diagnosis and surveillance of eye diseases.
Existing annotation systems are also in general desktop-based, lack custom surface definitions, and output data that would not necessarily be readily compatible with other pipelines in research. The necessity to possess a light and a web-based tool that enables the researcher to possess direct authority regarding the characterization of surfaces, order and the decharge of information is apparent.

1.5 Key System Capabilities
    • Web-based interface: it may be utilized in any modern browser (Firefox, Safari, Edge); it is not necessary to install it by the end users.
    • OCT Volume Loading: load DICOM and TIFF volumetric files (100-512 slices/ file, 1000-1024 pixels/ single frame)
    • Pan and Zoom: pan and zoom of high-resolution slices; annotations are not lost when the process is carried out.
    • Annotation Tools: free hand annotation (new feature, priority of the client 2), line/polygon keypoint annotation.
    • Annotation Palette: is able to add, delete, rearrange preset retinal surface labels (ILM, RPE, NFL etc.) and every annotation is linked to a surface label; every annotation is a list of points.
    • JSON Export: JSON annotations, based on defined schema; acceptable enough to be re-used by a client downstream.
    • Eric (US East Coast): the team will reserve meetings such that it accommodates the time zone difference between the EST and EDT.
2. Project Roadmap
2.1 Overview
The project will be carried out with the assumption of two sprints of ITECH3208 (Project 1), and can be extended in ITECH3209 (Project 2) at a later semester. The roadmap below identifies high-level sprint objectives, major deliverables and Definition of Done of each sprint outcome.
